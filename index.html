A little about me

I am passionate about applying biomedical engineering, computational methods, and artificial intelligence to real-world healthcare problems.

My interests span medical imaging, biosignal processing, machine learning, assistive technologies, and data-driven healthcare. I enjoy understanding a problem from both the biological and engineering perspectives and building practical solutions around it.

My academic projects have given me hands-on experience with physiological signals, image analysis, statistical analysis, hardware systems, MATLAB, Arduino, and machine learning.

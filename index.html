About Me

🩻 "A medical image is more than a picture. It is a spatial map of underlying biology."

That single principle defines how I approach Medical Imaging and Artificial Intelligence.

As a Biomedical Engineer, my focus lives in the space between image acquisition and biological discovery: how magnetic resonance (MRI) signals encode tissue properties, how advanced image-processing pipelines isolate structural patterns, and how quantitative AI models turn raw pixels into clinical measurements.

The Processing & Intelligence Pipeline
Biological Physics → MRI Acquisition → Image Data → Processing & Segmentation → Quantification → AI / Intelligence → Clinical Insight

Core Technical Focus

  • MRI & Medical Imaging: Anatomical, functional, and quantitative MRI protocols and signal interpretation.
  • Image Processing & Segmentation: Multi-contrast alignment, artifact reduction, denoising, and deep-learning-driven organ/tissue segmentation.
  • Quantitative Analysis: Feature extraction, tissue characterization, and reproducible biomarker measurement.
  • AI & Machine Learning: Model training, computer vision, and deep learning for clinical decision support.

Computational Toolkit

  • Languages & Analytics: Python, SQL, NumPy, Pandas, SciPy
  • Medical Image Analysis: OpenCV, PyTorch, Scikit-Image, SimpleITK, DICOM & NIfTI handling
  • Methodologies: Cellular bioimage analysis, image segmentation, signal normalization, and quantitative feature extraction
